# ADR 014: Operational adjustments are not a general ledger

Accepted. Freight, commission, platform fees, rebates and allocated operating
expense are management facts, not accounting vouchers. They do not assert invoice,
bank, tax or statutory evidence. Unallocated cost remains visible instead of
being treated as zero. Agents may explain and link these records but cannot post
or reverse them; those commands require an authorized human BusinessSession.

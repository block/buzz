# Business Core B2

B2 implements the first authoritative trade loop: draft sales order, atomic
full reservation at confirmation, optional hold, partial shipment, append-only
inventory/cost movement, operational receivable, customer receipt and
allocation. B3 now owns purchasing and operational payables. B2 still
deliberately excludes supplier invoices, bank
reconciliation, tax, general ledger and complete order profit.

## Authorities and invariants

- B1 enterprise user, role, permission and six-dimensional scope checks are
  reused for every read and command.
- `inventory_movements` is quantity/value truth; `inventory_balances` is its
  lockable projection. Negative on-hand or available stock is forbidden.
- Confirmation is fail-closed and all-or-nothing: balances are locked in stable
  warehouse/SKU order and every line must be reserved.
- The browser confirmation preview groups demand by warehouse/SKU and reports
  current on-hand, reserved, available, expected reservation and shortage. It
  also reports whether the current actor has confirmation permission. The
  preview is advisory: the confirmation transaction always locks and checks
  balances again so a concurrent reservation cannot oversell inventory.
- Shipment confirmation consumes reservations, posts quantity/value at the
  moving-average snapshot, updates the order and creates one operational
  receivable in the same transaction.
- Shipment drafts are confirmed only from a permission-aware release preview.
  It exposes live reservation, on-hand, reserved and moving-average cost per
  line, plus expected sales, frozen cost, operational receivable and due date.
  Missing cost, insufficient inventory, order hold/state and missing permission
  all block release. The preview is advisory; confirmation repeats every
  authoritative check under transaction locks.
- The browser shipment entry surface lists only confirmed, unheld orders with
  an open reservation in the actor's scope. Existing draft shipment quantities
  reduce the displayed shippable remainder. Shipment creation locks the
  reservation and repeats that check, preventing concurrent duplicate drafts
  from over-allocating the same reservation.
- Allocation facts are append-only. A correction inserts a reversal fact.
  Receipts must be fully deallocated before reversal; receivables must be open
  before shipment reversal.
- Every successful transition writes audit and outbox records in the same
  PostgreSQL transaction and supports command idempotency.
- Money and quantity use `NUMERIC(24,6)`/Rust Decimal and JSON decimal strings.

## State machines

Sales orders: `draft → confirmed → partially_shipped → shipped`; remaining
quantity may be cancelled. Hold is an orthogonal `none ↔ manual_review_hold`.
Shipments: `draft → confirmed → reversed`. Receivables: `open →
partially_settled → settled`, or `reversed` after an eligible shipment reversal.
Receipts: `draft → confirmed → partially_allocated → fully_allocated`; after
allocation reversals they may become `reversed`.

## Interfaces and verification

Service reads use `/v1/*` with service authentication and actor headers. Human
commands use `/api/v1/*` with BusinessSession, CSRF, exact Origin, idempotency
and expected version. The independent `apps/business-web` provides full and
`/embed` routes; Business Dock resolves strict `biz://` resources to them.
Draft orders are confirmed only from the detail page after reading
`GET /api/v1/sales-orders/{id}/confirmation-preview`; list views do not expose
a direct confirmation command.
Sales shipment drafts use
`GET /api/v1/shipments/draft-options` to select an eligible order, warehouse
and remaining order lines without exposing internal UUID entry fields.
Draft shipment confirmation is available only from its detail page after
reading `GET /api/v1/shipments/{id}/confirmation-preview`; list views link to
that evidence surface instead of exposing a direct confirmation command.
`business-read-api` uses Business Core for B2 tools and has no production
fixture fallback. Run `just business-b2-check` plus the disposable PostgreSQL
flow in the service README. Reconciliation must return no rows before rollout.

## B3 handoff

B3 reuses the append-only inventory movement authority. Purchase receipts
increase quantity and value and update future moving-average cost; they never
rewrite a B2 shipment cost snapshot. B2 receivables and B3 payables remain
distinct from invoice and accounting authorities.

## B4 handoff

B4 projects confirmed and reversed shipment outbox events into immutable net
revenue and product-cost facts. The historical cost is always B2's frozen
shipment cost snapshot; current inventory moving average is never substituted.

# Business Core B3

B3 completes the purchasing-side operational loop: purchase order, partial
goods receipt, provisional purchase cost, moving-average inventory update,
operational payable, supplier payment and append-only allocation/reversal.

Purchase order lifecycle and receiving status are separate. Confirmation is a
commitment only. Goods receipt confirmation atomically posts inventory facts,
updates the balance and PO projection, creates one operational payable, and
writes audit/outbox. Supplier invoices, input-tax decisions, three-way match,
bank proof, general ledger and multi-currency settlement are intentionally not
represented.

Human writes require BusinessSession, exact Origin, CSRF, idempotency key,
expected version, rate limit and current B1 authorization. Agent/service
identities remain read-only. Run `just business-b3-check` and the disposable
PostgreSQL command in `services/business-core/README.md`.

The browser purchase-order surface uses scoped master-data selectors for legal
entity, supplier, business unit, SKU, receiving warehouse and unit of measure.
`GET /api/v1/purchase-orders/entry-options` exposes create/update permission and
the authoritative draft payload for editing; users never enter internal UUIDs
or command JSON. The UI reports net purchase amount, tax and gross total before
the draft is saved. Backend validation remains authoritative for scope,
duplicate SKU/warehouse rows, decimal precision and amount rules.
Draft purchase orders are confirmed only from their detail page after
`GET /api/v1/purchase-orders/{id}/confirmation-preview` reports the supplier,
delivery warehouses, dates, line completeness, order totals and current
confirmation permission. The confirmation transaction repeats active supplier,
business-unit, SKU, product, unit-of-measure and warehouse validation. Purchase
order lists therefore link to this review instead of exposing a direct confirm
command.

The browser goods-receipt surface reads eligible confirmed purchase-order
lines from `GET /api/v1/goods-receipts/draft-options`. Existing receipt drafts
reduce the displayed receivable quantity, and receipt creation locks purchase
lines before repeating the same allocation check. Draft receipts are confirmed
only from their detail page after
`GET /api/v1/goods-receipts/{id}/confirmation-preview` reports permission,
remaining purchase quantity, provisional inventory cost, projected on-hand,
inventory value and moving-average cost, expected operational payable and due
date. Confirmation repeats the checks under transaction locks.

B4 now consumes B2 shipment outbox events and B3-stabilized product cost history
to produce management profitability. B3 remains the authority for provisional
purchase cost and payables; it does not become a general ledger.

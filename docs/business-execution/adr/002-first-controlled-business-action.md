# ADR 002: First Controlled Business Action

## Status

Deferred; no action selected.

## Decision

Exactly one future pilot may be selected only from real capability metadata. It
must be one object, Low or controlled Medium risk, reversible with a proven
inverse/compensation, non-financial, non-batch, Staging-supported, idempotent,
Expected-Version protected, Postcondition-readable, permission checked and
covered by formal approvers plus Step-up.

## Consequences

The illustrative manual-review hold is not selected merely because it appears
in the requirements. High/Critical, customer-wide, inventory, payment,
invoice, accounting and tax actions are excluded from the first pilot.
